## What's new

### Fixed
- fix(player): converted playback (E-AC3 / AC3 / DTS audio, HEVC video) could stop after about 10 seconds and restart from the beginning on resume; the conversion now keeps its output in a temporary file so the player can continue where it left off instead of restarting it
- fix(player): the conversion stops and its temporary file is deleted shortly after the player is closed or another title is opened, and it never runs more than 256 MB ahead of playback

### Changed
- chore(player): ffmpeg errors are written to `hurricanetv-ffmpeg.log` in the temp folder to help diagnose playback problems
